Data integrity policy

No invented figures. Missing is not zero. Spending on charitable activities is not the same as direct cash grants to beneficiaries. CEO pay may not be separately disclosed in statutory accounts.

England and Wales charity records and annual return figures have been imported. Scotland, Northern Ireland and more detailed comparisons remain under development. We will publish the reporting year, regulator, source and extraction method for every figure.
